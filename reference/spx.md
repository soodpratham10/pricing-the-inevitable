# Fractional-age survival within one year

Fractional-age survival within one year

## Usage

``` r
spx(qx, s, assumption = "udd")
```

## Arguments

- qx:

  One-year death probability.

- s:

  Fraction of the year, between 0 and 1.

- assumption:

  "udd" (uniform distribution of deaths), "cf" (constant force) or
  "balducci".

## Value

The probability of surviving the fraction s of the year.

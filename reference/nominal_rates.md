# Nominal rates of interest and discount

Nominal rates of interest and discount

## Usage

``` r
nominal_rates(i, m)
```

## Arguments

- i:

  Effective annual interest rate.

- m:

  Number of payments a year.

## Value

A list with `im` (nominal interest), `dm` (nominal discount), `alpha`
and `beta` (the UDD m-thly annuity adjustment factors).
